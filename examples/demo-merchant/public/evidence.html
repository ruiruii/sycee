Records only. No scoring, no custody, no fund movement.

Each paid call signs a merchant receipt. This page recomputes its fingerprint and checks the NEAR anchor. It does not submit a payment and it does not hold a key.

The fingerprint is sha256(canonical JSON of { vsr, signature }), the same bytes verify.mjs hashes. The raw x402 payment is not in the receipt. result.consumed on a merchant receipt stays unknown.
